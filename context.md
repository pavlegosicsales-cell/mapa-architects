## Target Audience
Two distinct groups, and the home page has to speak to both:
1. **Corporate / institutional investors and developers** — retail chains, car importers, hotel
   groups, banks, insurers. They buy delivery capability: project management, standardisation,
   framework agreements, on-time on-budget.
2. **Private clients** — villas, apartments, family houses. They buy sensibility: light, place,
   material, the poetic register Predrag already writes in on Instagram.
